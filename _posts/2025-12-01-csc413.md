The aim of our project is to examine the types of energy used by each Canadian province and the corresponding greenhouse gas emissions by visualizing relevant data. As climate change becomes an increasingly pressing issue—impacting infrastructure, ecosystems, and human health—it is essential to analyze how renewable energy is distributed across provinces and territories. This analysis helps identify areas for improvement as Canada works toward becoming a more sustainable nation.

To support our findings, we created presentation materials and visual explanations using **Tinkercad**, which helped us design clear and engaging PowerPoint presentations. Additionally, we highlight the emissions produced by each province to better understand their environmental impact and identify where meaningful change is most needed.

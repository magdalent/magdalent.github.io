Created an ArcGIS StoryMap examining the catastrophic impacts of the Sudanese Civil War on civilians. The project highlights displacement, violence, and humanitarian access challenges in what is currently the **largest humanitarian crisis in the world**.

This work was completed as part of the **Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team (HOT) Mentorship Program**, integrating humanitarian mapping, data storytelling, and conflict-sensitive cartographic design.
